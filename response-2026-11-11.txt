Response to reviewers

- "My main concern is the scope of the manuscript and the actionability of
  several tips for its stated audience." I have added clarifying notes in
  several places to distinguish what small teams can reasonably be expected
  to do from what they should ask IT to do (if available).

- "Tips 11 and 12 broaden the manuscript from technical disaster prep into
  leadership." I have rewritten the first paragraph of Tip 11 to make the
  shift of focus explicit. (I believe very strongly that the human aspects
  of disaster management are as important as the IT aspects, and so have
  opted to retain these tips.)

- "On the 'Questions for Support': what to do in case there is no such support
  available on site?" Addressed with several comments.

- "Please consider adding web links into in the running text rather than having
  both footnotes and numbered references in case the journal allows this." I am
  using PLOs's own style file here.

- "CRAN is a mirrored network, so there is no single failure point once a
  package is published." Noted, and comment added.

- "Data repositories do not primarily serve as platform for data backup, but for
  data publishing, hence the DOI." Thank you for pointing this out - I've added
  a clarifying note.

- "The note field in Tip 3 seems redundant if the 3-2-1 rule is adhered to."
  Agreed and addressed.

- "Tip 6 seems to be a rule to monitor tip 1." I've added a backward reference
  from the former to the latter.

- "In case offboarding is suggested, I think mentioning onboarding is similarly
  critical." Agreed - I've added a couple of sentences.

- "Please consider refining Tip 8. This rule is not actionable at all for
  readers subordinate to local IT support." Clarifying note added.

- "If a research software team is small with few members and just produces
  analysis code and some packages, the 12 tips seem disproportionate. Please
  consider adding a small acknowledgement of this." With respect, I disagree
  with the reviewer on this point.
